Affiliate Disclosure

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

GymTranning is committed to full transparency about our affiliate relationships. This disclosure explains how affiliate links work and how they support our site.

1. What Are Affiliate Links

Some links on GymTranning are affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may receive a small commission at no additional cost to you. This commission helps us maintain and improve the site, create new content, and keep our resources free for all users.

2. How We Use Affiliate Links

We may include affiliate links in the following types of content:

  • Product recommendations in articles and guides
  • Equipment recommendations in workout guides
  • Supplement reviews and nutrition articles
  • Tool and resource recommendations

We only recommend products and services that we have researched and believe will provide value to our readers. We do not promote products solely for the purpose of earning commissions.

3. Impact on You

Using affiliate links has no impact on the price you pay. In fact, we sometimes negotiate exclusive discounts for our readers. The commission we earn does not increase the cost of any product or service you purchase through our links.

4. Editorial Integrity

Our content is never influenced by affiliate relationships. Writers and reviewers are not compensated based on affiliate sales. We maintain strict editorial independence and follow the guidelines outlined in our Editorial Policy.

If a product or service does not meet our standards, we will not recommend it regardless of any affiliate relationship. Our priority is always the trust and well-being of our readers.

5. Questions

If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships or how we use affiliate links, please contact us. We are happy to provide additional transparency about any specific recommendation or link on the site.
