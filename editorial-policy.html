Editorial Policy

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

At GymTranning, we are committed to producing fitness content that is accurate, evidence-based, and transparent. Our editorial process ensures every article meets rigorous standards before publication.

1. Our Mission

GymTranning exists to make evidence-based fitness education accessible to everyone. Every piece of content we publish serves this mission by translating exercise science into practical, actionable guidance.

2. Content Standards

All content published on GymTranning adheres to the following standards:

  • Evidence-Based: Claims are supported by peer-reviewed research, established exercise science principles, or expert consensus.
  • Transparent: We clearly distinguish between established science, emerging research, and expert opinion.
  • Practical: Information is presented in actionable terms that readers can apply to their training.
  • Current: Content is reviewed and updated regularly to reflect the latest research and best practices.
  • Balanced: We present multiple perspectives when the science is inconclusive and avoid overgeneralization.

3. Review Process

Every article published on GymTranning undergoes a structured multi-step review process:

  1. Research & Drafting: Our writers, who are certified fitness professionals, research the topic using peer-reviewed journals and authoritative sources.
  2. Technical Review: A subject matter expert reviews the content for accuracy, completeness, and alignment with current exercise science.
  3. Medical Review: Content that touches on health, injury prevention, or medical topics is reviewed by our medical reviewer, Dr. Michael Roberts.
  4. Editorial Review: Our editorial team reviews for clarity, readability, and adherence to our content standards.
  5. Final Approval: The content is approved for publication by the Head of Content.

After publication, content is reviewed on a regular cycle and updated as needed to reflect new research or changes in best practices.

4. Sourcing & Citations

We prioritize sources that meet the following criteria:

  • Peer-reviewed studies published in reputable journals (e.g., Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research, Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise)
  • Position stands from authoritative organizations (e.g., American College of Sports Medicine, National Strength and Conditioning Association)
  • Government health agencies (e.g., NIH, CDC, WHO)
  • Expert consensus from qualified professionals with relevant credentials

When the available science is limited or inconclusive, we clearly communicate this to our readers and explain the basis for our recommendations.

5. Updates & Corrections

GymTranning is committed to keeping content current. Our content review cycle includes:

  • Scheduled Reviews: All articles are reviewed at least once every 12 months.
  • Prompt Corrections: When errors are identified, we correct them promptly and note the correction at the bottom of the article.
  • Version Tracking: Each article includes a "Last Updated" date in its metadata to reflect the most recent review.

If you identify an error in our content, please contact us so we can investigate and make corrections as needed.

6. Conflict of Interest

GymTranning maintains strict editorial independence. Our content is not influenced by advertisers, sponsors, or affiliate partnerships. We clearly disclose any affiliate relationships in our Affiliate Disclosure.

Our writers and reviewers are required to disclose any relationships that could be perceived as influencing their work. Content recommendations are based solely on the evidence and the best interests of our readers.
