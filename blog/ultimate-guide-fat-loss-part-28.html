    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the ideal calorie deficit for fat loss?

    The ideal deficit for most people is 300-500 calories below maintenance, or roughly 10-20% of total daily energy expenditure. This range promotes steady fat loss of 0.5-1 lb per week while preserving muscle mass and supporting energy levels for training. Beginners can start at the lower end and adjust based on results.

    How do I know if my deficit is too aggressive?

    Signs of an overly aggressive deficit include persistent fatigue, irritability, disrupted sleep, loss of strength in the gym, hair loss, irregular menstrual cycles, constant hunger, and a rapid drop in scale weight exceeding 2 lb per week. If you experience these, increase your calories by 100-200 per day and monitor how you feel.

    Can I train hard while in a calorie deficit?

    Yes, but training performance may decrease slightly, especially for high-intensity work. Prioritize compound lifts, keep volume moderate, and ensure protein intake is adequate (1.6-2.2 g per kg of body weight). Consider timing carbohydrates around workouts to fuel performance, and schedule deload weeks or diet breaks every 8-12 weeks to manage accumulated fatigue.
