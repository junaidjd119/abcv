Full Issue Registry (with fixes & ranking impact)

CRITICAL-1 — Invalid JSON-LD on correcting-posture-exercises.html
Affected: /blog/correcting-posture-exercises.html (3 VideoObject blocks)
Problem: VideoObject descriptions contain unescaped double quotes around "Tech Neck" (Fixing "Tech Neck"), breaking JSON.parse for 3 blocks.
Evidence: 858 JSON-LD blocks parsed; 3 failures — all on this page (ConvertFrom-Json error: "':' or '}' expected").
SEO Impact: Google discards the 3 video schemas; page loses video rich-result eligibility. Search Console will show "Unparsable structured data" errors.
Exact Fix: Replace "Tech Neck" with \"Tech Neck\" (or use typographic quotes) in the 3 description strings, then re-validate with Rich Results Test.
Estimated ranking impact: Low on rankings, but blocks 3 video rich results; fix within 1 deploy.
HIGH-1 — Homepage video wall (92 videos) has no VideoObject and 27 videos missing from video-sitemap
Affected: https://gymtranning.com/ — 92 <video> tags, incl. 27 MVI_*.mp4 camera files absent from video-sitemap.xml
Problem: Homepage embeds 92 self-hosted videos with no schema markup; 27 of them (raw camera clips in kalai/, leg/, shoulder/, tri scps/, shoulders/, triceps/ folders) appear nowhere in the video sitemap.
Evidence: 92 video srcs extracted from homepage HTML; cross-referenced against 120 video-sitemap content_loc entries → 27 unmatched. All 92 files return 200.
SEO Impact: Missed video indexing surface for the homepage; raw camera files have no titles/thumbnails — poor video SEO candidates.
Exact Fix: Either add VideoObject for the 5–10 featured homepage videos, or curate the homepage wall down to videos already in the sitemap; remove or properly title/thumbnail the 27 MVI_* clips; add homepage video entries to video-sitemap.xml.
Estimated ranking impact: Low–moderate (video results are a secondary surface; the homepage is the strongest page on the site).
MEDIUM-1 — VideoObject missing "duration" on 103 pages
Affected: 103 of 134 pages with VideoObject (e.g., all ultimate-guide series pages)
Problem: VideoObject blocks lack the recommended duration property (ISO 8601).
Evidence: Schema audit: 408 VideoObject blocks; duration present on only 31 pages, absent on 103.
SEO Impact: Video rich results still possible (duration is recommended, not required) but completeness signals weaker.
Exact Fix: Add "duration": "PT5M" (or actual lengths) to every VideoObject — matches video-sitemap which already declares PT5M.
Estimated ranking impact: Low; improves video rich-result eligibility and consistency.
MEDIUM-2 — Contact form has placeholder Formspree ID (form is dead)
Affected: /contact.html
Problem: Form posts to https://formspree.io/f/your_form_id — submissions fail. Also weakens E-E-A-T (Contact page is broken).
Evidence: Live HTML: <form action="https://formspree.io/f/your_form_id">
SEO Impact: Direct E-E-A-T signal damage; Google guidelines require a working contact method. Users cannot reach the site owner.
Exact Fix: Register a real Formspree endpoint (or mailto/alternate contact method) and replace your_form_id; verify a test submission.
Estimated ranking impact: Low–moderate for E-E-A-T on a fitness (YMYL-adjacent) niche.
MEDIUM-3 — Thumbnails are 128×104 px — ineligible for Google Discover / weak social previews
Affected: All 134 thumbnails + all og:image tags sitewide
Problem: Every og:image points to a 128×104 JPEG. Google Discover requires images ≥1200 px wide; social platforms render tiny previews.
Evidence: Downloaded 3 thumbnails and parsed JPEG SOF markers → 128×104 px (20–39 KB each).
SEO Impact: Blocks Google Discover eligibility entirely (Discover score 35%); degrades CTR on Facebook/LinkedIn/WhatsApp.
Exact Fix: Regenerate thumbnails at ≥1200×630 (ideally 1920×1080) and update poster + og:image + thumbnail_loc in video-sitemap. Add a dedicated 1200×630 social/og image per article if possible.
Estimated ranking impact: Low for SERP, high for Discover/social surface (traffic source expansion).
MEDIUM-4 — No compression; homepage payload 330 KB
Affected: Homepage (330 KB), blog.html (197 KB), gym-empire-arcade.html (197 KB), all HTML (5.1 MB raw, no gzip/brotli)
Problem: GitHub Pages does not compress; the homepage ships 187 KB inline JS + 92 video tags + 10 KB inline CSS unminified raw.
Evidence: Header inspection: no Content-Encoding on HTML; Content-Length 337,643 (homepage).
SEO Impact: Slower LCP/FCP on mobile for the 3 heavy pages; desktop-first health fine. INP risk from 187 KB inline JS on homepage.
Exact Fix: Move inline JS to external files, prune the 92-video markup (virtualize with JS templates), minify; if possible use Cloudflare in front for Brotli. Homepage target ≤150 KB.
Estimated ranking impact: Low (desktop), moderate for mobile CWV on homepage only.
