Biological friction causes excuses. Understanding this neurological connection helps prepare your mind to execute heavy compound lifts with focus.

Concentrating on muscle contraction (mind-muscle connection) recruits more motor units. Maximizing the mechanical tension experienced by target muscle fibers. This mindset shift helps you embrace the discomfort of training as a stimulator for growth.
