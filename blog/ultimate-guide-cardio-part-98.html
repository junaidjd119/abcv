    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is Sports Performance Conditioning?

    Sports Performance Conditioning is a cardiovascular training method that improves heart health, lung capacity, and endurance through targeted exercise protocols.

    How does Sports Performance Conditioning benefit heart health?

    Studies show Sports Performance Conditioning strengthens the cardiovascular system, lowers resting heart rate, and improves overall cardiorespiratory fitness.

    Is Sports Performance Conditioning safe for beginners?

    Yes, beginners can start at lower intensities and gradually progress. Those with heart conditions should consult a doctor first.
